Shikimori Bots — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Shikimori Bots may process information when its Discord bots are installed or used.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the specific bot and enabled features, Shikimori Bots may process information such as:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and bot IDs.
  • Server configuration and bot settings.
  • Command usage and information required to respond to commands.
  • Moderation, security, warning, mute, ban, or logging information when those features are enabled.
  • Information contained in Discord events that the applicable bot is technically permitted to receive through Discord's API.

3. Why Information Is Processed

Information may be processed to operate bot features, save server configuration, provide moderation and security functionality, generate logs requested by administrators, prevent abuse, maintain reliability, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Messages and Content

Not every bot stores message content. Where a feature requires message or event information, processing is limited to what is necessary for that feature and the bot's configuration. Server administrators should review the documentation or configuration of the specific Shikimori Bots service they use.

5. Storage and Retention

Retention depends on the specific bot, its database, enabled features, and server configuration. Logs and moderation records may remain for as long as the relevant feature is configured to retain them.

6. Sharing

Shikimori Bots does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, database, infrastructure, or other service providers when necessary to operate the applicable bot.

7. Discord

Discord independently processes information under its own policies. Shikimori Bots does not control Discord's data practices.

8. Server Administrator Responsibility

Server owners and administrators are responsible for configuring logging, moderation, and data-retention features appropriately and for informing their community when required by applicable rules or law.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information handled by the services. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Privacy Requests

For questions or privacy-related requests concerning Shikimori Bots, contact the project administrator through the official support channel associated with the project.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the services, features, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
