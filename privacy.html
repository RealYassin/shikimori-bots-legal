Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Shikimori Bots may process information when it is used in Discord servers.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on enabled features, the bot may process Discord identifiers and server information needed to perform commands and security or moderation functions, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message or moderation-related information, and configuration settings.

3. Why Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot features, enforce configured security and moderation rules, maintain configuration, generate requested logs, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Storage and Retention

Data retention depends on the bot's configuration and the features enabled by a server administrator. Server administrators are responsible for configuring logging and retention features appropriately.

5. Sharing

Shikimori Shield does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers required to operate the bot, where applicable.

6. Your Choices

Server administrators can remove the bot from their server. For a data or privacy request, contact the Shikimori Shield administrator through the official support channel associated with the bot.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the service, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
